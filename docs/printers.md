# Supported printers

Scan to Me works with network all-in-one printers whose control panel offers
**Scan to Computer** and that speak one of the protocols below. The printer and
your iPhone or iPad must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

## Verified

| Printer | Protocol | Status |
|---|---|---|
| HP Photosmart 6510 e-All-in-One (B211a) | LEDM | Works: PDF and JPEG, multi-page from the glass |

## Expected to work

Printers in these families use the same protocol as a verified model, but
nobody has confirmed them with the iPhone app yet. If you try one, please
[tell us how it went](https://github.com/olibar/scan-to-me/issues/new?template=printer-request.yml).

| Family | Protocol | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HP inkjet all-in-ones, roughly 2010-2020 (Photosmart, OfficeJet, OfficeJet Pro, ENVY, Deskjet) | LEDM | OfficeJet Pro 9010 verified with the desktop version, including the document feeder |

## Not supported yet

* Other printer brands. Their Scan to Computer buttons use protocols of their
  own; support may come later. Requests help decide which comes first.
* Printers without a network connection (USB only).

## Ask for your printer
