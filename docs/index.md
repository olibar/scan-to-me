Scan to Me turns your iPhone or iPad into a destination for the **Scan to
Computer** button of your network all-in-one printer. Press Scan on the
printer, pick your phone from the list, and the pages arrive over Wi-Fi as a
PDF or as JPEG images.

No cables, no cloud, no account. Scans go straight from the printer to your
device.

## How to use it

1. Open Scan to Me, go to **Printers** and tap **+**. Pick your printer from
   the list, or enter its IP address. The first time, allow **Local Network**
   access when iOS asks.
2. In **Settings**, choose the name shown on the printer, the format, the
   resolution and the folder to save into.
3. On the **Listen** tab, tap **Start listening**. Your printer shows "Ready".
4. On the printer: **Scan > Computer >** your phone's name **>** Start. For a
   multi-page PDF, answer "scan another page" on the printer, then choose
   Done. With paper in the document feeder, the whole stack becomes one PDF.

Your phone appears on the printer **only while Scan to Me is open and
listening**: iOS does not let apps listen in the background. The screen stays
on while listening.

## Troubleshooting

* **The printer says no computer was found.** Start listening in the app
  *before* opening the Scan menu on the printer, and check that the printer
  shows "Ready" in the app.
* **No printer found when adding one.** In iOS Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Local Network, allow Scan to Me. Or add the printer by its IP address (shown
  in the printer's network settings).
* **Something else.** On the Listen tab, the magnifier icon opens the activity
  log; the Copy button puts it on the clipboard. Send it with your question.
